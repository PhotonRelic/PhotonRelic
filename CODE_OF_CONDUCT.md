# Code of conduct

Be decent to each other. That covers most of it; the rest is detail.

**Expected.** Treat everyone in this project's issues, pull requests and
discussions with respect. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good
intent, give and take review as help rather than judgement, and remember
that a maintainer saying no to a change is not a verdict on you.

**Not tolerated.** Harassment, personal attacks, slurs, sexualised language
or imagery, threats, stalking, publishing someone's private information, or
sustained disruption. Nor is any of this acceptable off-project when it is
aimed at a participant because of their involvement here.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues
and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders
from the project. They will say why when they do.

**Reporting.** Email [security@photonrelic.com](mailto:security@photonrelic.com).
Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered. If the
problem involves a maintainer, say so; it will be handled by someone else.

This applies to everyone who takes part, maintainers included.
